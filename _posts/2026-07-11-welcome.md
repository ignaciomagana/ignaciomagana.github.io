Welcome! This is the new home for my research on black holes, gravitational
waves and the expanding Universe.

I plan to use this space for short, informal notes: explaining a result in
plain language, sharing a method or a plot I'm excited about, or pointing to a
new paper or piece of code. If you're looking for the formal record, the
[publications](/publications/) page and my
[INSPIRE page](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1902658) have everything.

A few themes you'll see me come back to:

- **Measuring how fast the Universe expands.** The gravitational waves from a
  merger tell us how far away it is, without the usual chain of stepwise
  distance measurements. I'm especially interested in mergers seen only in
  gravitational waves, with no light, and in how galaxy surveys like DESI help
  pin down where they happened.
- **Letting the data speak.** Flexible models that let the data set the shape
  can reveal features in the range of black hole masses that fixed formulas
  miss, like a possible feature near 70 times the mass of the Sun in recent
  catalogs.
- **Gravity as a laboratory.** From gravitational-wave signals bent by galaxies
  to tests of whether gravity leaks into extra dimensions, mergers are sharp
  tests of gravity across the Universe.
