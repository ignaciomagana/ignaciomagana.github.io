Welcome! This is the new home for my research on gravitational-wave cosmology,
compact-binary populations, and gravitational-wave lensing.

I plan to use this space for short, informal notes — explaining a result in
plain language, sharing a method or a plot I'm excited about, or pointing to a
new paper or piece of code. If you're looking for the formal record, the
[publications](/publications/) page and my
[INSPIRE-HEP profile](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1902658) have everything.

A few themes you'll see me come back to:

- **Standard sirens and the Hubble tension.** Gravitational waves let us measure
  cosmic distances without a distance ladder. I'm especially interested in
  *dark sirens* — mergers with no electromagnetic counterpart — and how galaxy
  surveys like DESI sharpen what they can tell us.
- **Letting the data speak.** Non-parametric, data-driven population models can
  reveal structure in the black-hole mass spectrum that parametric models miss —
  like the feature near 70 M⊙ that turned up in recent catalogs.
- **Gravity as a laboratory.** From gravitational-wave lensing to tests of the
  number of spacetime dimensions, mergers are precision probes of gravity on
  the largest scales.
