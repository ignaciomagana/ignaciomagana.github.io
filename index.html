{% include binary.html %}About

I am a Research Physicist at Carnegie Mellon University, where I was a McWilliams Postdoctoral Fellow (2023–2026). I study merging black hole pairs, using their gravitational waves (ripples in spacetime) to learn where they form, which galaxies they merge in, and how fast the Universe expands. My work found evidence for the start of a gap: a range of masses where stars are expected to blow apart and leave no black hole. I lead the DESI galaxy survey's measurement of the expansion rate from these mergers and built the open-source software darksirens. During my PhD at the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee, I helped develop gwcosmo and led the LIGO–Virgo collaboration's measurement of the expansion rate from GW190814.
