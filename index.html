{% include binary.html %}About

I am a Research Physicist in the Department of Physics at Carnegie Mellon University, where I was a McWilliams Postdoctoral Fellow from 2023 to 2026. I study where binary black holes merge and how long after star formation they do so, combining gravitational-wave population inference with DESI galaxy spectroscopy. My nonparametric population models found evidence for the onset of the pair-instability mass gap, and my spectral-siren analyses measure the Hubble constant from the black-hole mass distribution. I lead the DESI dark-siren analysis and built darksirens, a GPU-accelerated framework for joint population and cosmology inference. During my PhD at the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee, I co-developed gwcosmo and led the LIGO–Virgo Hubble-constant analysis of GW190814.
