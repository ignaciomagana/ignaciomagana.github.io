Research

Reading the Universe in gravitational waves

Every compact-binary merger LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA detects is both a ruler and a fossil — encoding how fast the Universe expands and how black holes and neutron stars come to be. My work turns catalogs of these mergers into statements about cosmology, populations, and gravity itself.
