{% include binary.html %}Software

  • darksirens (designer and developer): software that runs on graphics cards to work out the properties of black hole pairs and the expansion rate of the Universe together. github.com/ignaciomagana/darksirens-core
  • gwcat: prepares the published LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA measurements of each merger, and the simulated signals that show what the detectors can and cannot see, for use in darksirens. github.com/ignaciomagana/gwcat
  • tinyns: a small, fast program for fitting models to data and comparing them. github.com/ignaciomagana/tinyns
  • gwcosmo (co-developer): the LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA code for measuring the expansion of the Universe with gravitational waves. git.ligo.org/lscsoft/gwcosmo

The full record is on the publications page; code is on GitHub.
